Free financial and business calculators

Docutoolbox's Financial & Business Calculators cover the numbers that matter most for loans, savings, taxes, and business planning. All tools run entirely in your browser using JavaScript, nothing you enter is ever uploaded to a server.

How to choose the right tool

  • Working out a loan repayment schedule -> Loan Calculator
  • Finding a monthly installment for a loan -> EMI Calculator
  • Estimating a home loan with taxes and insurance -> Mortgage Calculator
  • Projecting investment growth over time -> Compound Interest Calculator
  • Quick interest calculations -> Simple Interest Calculator
  • Adding or removing sales tax -> VAT / GST Calculator
  • Comparing purchasing power across years -> Inflation Calculator
  • Pricing a product correctly -> Profit Margin & Markup Calculator
  • Finding how much you need to sell to break even -> Break-Even Point Calculator
  • Evaluating an investment's return -> NPV Calculator
  • Setting a freelance hourly rate -> Freelance Hourly Rate Calculator
  • Planning toward a savings target -> Savings Goal Calculator

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use these calculators?

No, every tool is free with no sign-up, no email, and no limits on use.

Is my financial data stored or sent anywhere?

No, all calculations happen locally in your browser using JavaScript, nothing you enter is transmitted to a server or stored.

Are these calculators a substitute for financial advice?

No, they provide general estimates for informational purposes, consult a qualified financial advisor, accountant, or lender for decisions specific to your situation.

What is the Alphabetical Order Sorter?

This tool sorts a list of lines into alphabetical order, ascending (A to Z) or descending (Z to A), with options to treat uppercase and lowercase differently and to remove duplicate lines as part of the sort.

How to use it

  1. Paste your list into the box, one item per line.
  2. Choose A → Z or Z → A.
  3. Optionally enable case sensitivity or duplicate removal.
  4. Click Sort text and copy the result.

Common uses

  • Sorting a list of names, products, or keywords alphabetically
  • Organizing a bibliography or reference list
  • Preparing a sorted list for a dropdown menu or database
  • Cleaning up an unsorted export from a spreadsheet or CMS

Frequently asked questions

How are numbers and symbols sorted?

Standard alphabetical sorting rules apply, numbers typically sort before letters, and symbols sort based on their character code, which may not always match intuitive expectations for mixed alphanumeric lists.

Does case sensitivity affect where a word is sorted, or just how duplicates are detected?

Both, with case sensitivity on, uppercase letters sort before lowercase (so "Zebra" comes before "apple"), and "Apple" and "apple" are treated as different values for duplicate detection.

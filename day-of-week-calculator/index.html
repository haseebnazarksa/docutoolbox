What is the Day of the Week Calculator?

This tool tells you which day of the week any date falls on - past, present, or future. Just pick a date and the result appears immediately, with no calendar flipping or mental math required.

It's useful for anyone booking events, researching history, or planning something that needs to land on a specific weekday.

How to use it

  1. Choose a date using the date picker.
  2. The calculator instantly shows the weekday (Monday, Tuesday, etc.) along with the full formatted date for confirmation.

How it works

The calculator uses the standard Gregorian calendar - the calendar used by most of the world today - to work out the weekday for any date, without needing an internet connection or external calendar service. It applies the same underlying date rules your device's operating system uses, which correctly account for leap years and the varying number of days in each month.

A brief note on the calendar system

The Gregorian calendar has been the internationally accepted civil calendar since it was introduced in 1582, gradually replacing the older Julian calendar in different countries over the following centuries. Because different countries switched at different times, calculating the weekday for very old historical dates (particularly before a given country's Gregorian adoption date) can require extra care - this calculator assumes the proleptic Gregorian calendar, meaning it applies Gregorian rules consistently even to dates before 1582.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for dates far in the future or the distant past?

Yes, the calculator can handle a very wide range of dates, both centuries into the past and far into the future.

Will the result differ depending on my time zone?

The calculation is based on the calendar date you select, not a specific moment in time, so the weekday result is the same regardless of time zone.

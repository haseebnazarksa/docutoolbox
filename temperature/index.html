Free temperature unit converters

Docutoolbox's Temperature Unit Converters cover every conversion between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin. Each converter has its own dedicated page for the fastest, most focused conversion experience, with live results, the exact formula, and a quick reference table.

How the converters work

Unlike simple length or weight conversions, temperature scales don't share a common zero point, so each conversion uses its own fixed formula rather than a single multiplier. Switching the "From" or "To" unit on any converter page instantly takes you to the matching dedicated converter, so you always land on the most relevant tool for your exact conversion.

Frequently asked questions

Are these conversions exact?

Yes, all temperature conversions here use the fixed, internationally standardized formulas between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin, results are mathematically exact.

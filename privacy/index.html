Privacy Policy

What this site does with your data

Docutoolbox's tools, including its PDF, image, text, calculator, and developer tools, run directly in your web browser using JavaScript. Text, images, files, and numbers you enter are never transmitted to a server, never stored, and never shared with anyone.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall traffic patterns. These tools may use cookies and collect general, non-personal information such as browser type and approximate location.

Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings.

Third-party links

This site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Its tools do not require or store any personal or identifying information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in how the site operates. Any changes will be posted on this page.

What is the Grade Calculator?

Many courses don't grade everything equally - a final exam might count for 40% of your grade while weekly quizzes only count for 10%. This calculator works out your overall weighted grade once you know the score and weight of each component.

How to use it

Add a row for each graded item (assignment, quiz, exam, project). Enter the score you got as a percentage, and how much that item is worth toward your final grade (its weight). The calculator combines everything into your current overall grade.

Common uses

  • Tracking your grade throughout a semester as new scores come in
  • Figuring out what score you'd need on a remaining assignment to hit a target grade
  • Double-checking a syllabus's weighting math

A note on total weight

If your weights don't add up to 100% yet (for example, because some assignments haven't happened), the "final grade" shown reflects only the items you've entered so far - treat it as your grade-to-date rather than your final course grade.

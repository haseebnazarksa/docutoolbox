Free speed unit converters

Docutoolbox's Speed Unit Converters cover every conversion between meters per second, kilometers per hour, miles per hour, feet per second, and knots. Each converter has its own dedicated page for the fastest, most focused conversion experience, with live results, the exact formula, and a quick reference table.

How the converters work

Speed conversions are ratio based, so every unit is defined as a fixed multiple of meters per second, the base unit used internally. Converting any value is simply multiplying by the source unit's factor and dividing by the target unit's factor. Switching the "From" or "To" unit on any converter page instantly takes you to the matching dedicated converter, so you always land on the most relevant tool for your exact conversion.

Frequently asked questions

Are these conversions exact?

Yes, all speed conversions here use fixed, mathematically defined ratios between meters per second, kilometers per hour, miles per hour, feet per second, and knots, results are always exact.

Which speed unit should I use?

Use kilometers per hour or miles per hour for everyday road speeds depending on your region, meters per second for scientific and physics work, feet per second for technical or ballistics calculations, and knots for maritime or aviation speeds.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No, every calculation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript, nothing is uploaded or stored.

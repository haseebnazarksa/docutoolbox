What is a gram?

A gram is a metric unit of mass equal to one-thousandth of a kilogram. It's the everyday unit for cooking ingredients, jewelry, medication doses, and postal weights.

What is a microgram?

A microgram is a very small metric unit of mass equal to one-millionth of a gram. It's used for vitamin and mineral dosages, hormone levels, and other trace amounts.

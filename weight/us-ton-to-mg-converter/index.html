What is a US ton?

The US ton (short ton) is a unit of mass used mainly in the United States, equal to 2,000 pounds. It's common in freight, construction materials, and vehicle weight ratings.

What is a milligram?

A milligram is a small metric unit of mass equal to one-thousandth of a gram. It's mainly used for medication dosages, nutrition labels, and laboratory measurements.

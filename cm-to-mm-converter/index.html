Base conversion values

1 millimeter = 0.1 cm

How to convert centimeters to millimeters

To convert a value from centimeters to millimeters, multiply it by 10. This is a fixed, internationally standardized conversion factor, so the result is always mathematically exact, never an estimate.

Formula

mm = cm × 10

Worked examples

Example 1: Convert 20 cm to mm
20 cm × 10 = 200 mm

Example 2: Convert 8 cm to mm
8 cm × 10 = 80 mm

Example 3: Convert 100 cm to mm
100 cm × 10 = 1000 mm

What is a centimeter?

A centimeter is a metric unit of length equal to one-hundredth of a meter. It's a common everyday unit for measuring height, small objects, and fabric.

What is a millimeter?

A millimeter is a small metric unit of length equal to one-thousandth of a meter. It's used for precise measurements in engineering, manufacturing, and everyday items like paper thickness or screw sizes.

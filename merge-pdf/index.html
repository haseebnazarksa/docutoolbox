What is the Merge PDF tool?

Merge PDF combines two or more PDF files into a single document — but instead of just stacking whole files on top of each other, it shows you a thumbnail of every individual page from every file you upload, so you can drag pages into the exact final order you want, page by page.

Everything happens locally in your browser — your files are never uploaded to a server. That means it works even with sensitive or private documents, and there's no waiting on an upload or download from a remote service.

How to use it

  1. Drag and drop your PDF files onto the upload area, or click it to browse your device — you can add several files at once.
  2. Every page from every file appears as a thumbnail in a grid, each labeled with a number showing its current position in the final merged PDF.
  3. Drag any page thumbnail to move it earlier or later — the numbers update live as you reorder.
  4. Click the × on any page to remove it entirely from the final document.
  5. Need more pages? Click "+ Add more PDFs" to upload additional files — their pages join the grid so you can slot them in anywhere.
  6. Click Merge PDFs and download the result.

Why page-level reordering matters

Simple "merge whole files" tools only let you decide which file comes first — but real documents are rarely that tidy. Maybe you need page 3 of one PDF to sit between pages 1 and 2 of another, or you want to drop a single blank page from a scan. Because this tool breaks every file down into individual page thumbnails, you have complete control over the final page order, not just the file order.

How it works technically

When you upload a file, the tool renders a small preview image of every page directly in your browser so you can see exactly what you're arranging. When you click merge, it copies each page — in the exact order shown in the grid — from its original source file into a brand-new PDF document, preserving the original text, vector graphics, and formatting of every page exactly as it was in the source file. No page is ever re-rendered as a flattened image in the final output; only the thumbnails you see while arranging are images.

Common uses

  • Combining scanned pages from multiple documents into one clean file, in the right order
  • Assembling a report from separately created sections, inserting a cover page at the front
  • Removing a blank or unwanted scanned page while merging several files together
  • Interleaving pages from two documents — for example, alternating front and back scans
  • Putting together an application packet where each document's pages need a specific order

File size and privacy

Because everything happens on your device, there's no upload wait time and no privacy risk from sending documents to a remote server. Very large files or a very high page count may take a little longer to render thumbnails for, depending on your device's available memory, since all processing happens locally rather than on a remote server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I mix pages from more than two PDFs?

Yes — upload as many PDFs as you need, and every page from every file appears together in one grid that you can freely reorder.

Will merging affect the quality of my pages?

No — the final merged PDF copies each page's original content directly, so text stays sharp and searchable and image quality is unchanged. Only the on-screen thumbnails are simplified preview images; the actual output keeps full original quality.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Password-protected files need to be unlocked first. Use the Unlock PDF tool to remove the password, then upload the unlocked file here.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The entire process — rendering thumbnails and merging — runs inside your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device or touch a server.

How to convert cm to feet and inches

To convert centimeters to feet and inches, first divide by 30.48 to get total feet, then take the decimal remainder and multiply by 12 to get the leftover inches. This calculator handles both steps automatically and shows the result in the familiar feet-and-inches format used for height and measurements.

Worked example

Convert 175 cm to feet and inches:
175 cm ÷ 2.54 = 68.90 total inches
68.90 ÷ 12 = 5 feet, remainder 8.90 inches
Result: 5 ft 8.90 in

Formula

Total inches = cm ÷ 2.54, then feet = total inches ÷ 12 (whole number), remaining inches = total inches mod 12.

What is a centimeter?

A centimeter is a metric unit of length equal to one-hundredth of a meter. It's a common everyday unit for measuring height, small objects, and fabric.

What are feet and inches?

Feet and inches are imperial units of length, with 12 inches making up 1 foot. Together they form the standard way height is expressed in the US and UK, for example "5 feet 9 inches."

Why is the inches value not a round number?

Centimeter and inch measurements rarely divide evenly, since the underlying conversion factor (2.54) isn't a whole number, small decimal remainders are normal and expected.

Free time unit converters

Docutoolbox's Time Unit Converters cover every conversion between twelve time units, from nanoseconds up to centuries. Each converter has its own dedicated page for the fastest, most focused conversion experience, with live results, the exact formula, and a quick reference table.

How the converters work

Every time unit is converted through a common base of seconds, so results stay accurate and consistent across the full range, from nanoseconds to centuries. Switching the "From" or "To" unit on any converter page instantly takes you to the matching dedicated converter, so you always land on the most relevant tool for your exact conversion.

  • Calculating meeting durations, work shifts, and daily schedules
  • Planning project deadlines, billing cycles, and subscription periods
  • Working out age, tenure, historical timelines, and long-term financial projections

Frequently asked questions

Are these conversions exact?

Yes, conversions between fixed-length units such as seconds, minutes, hours, days, and weeks are mathematically exact. Month, calendar year, decade, and century use standard average lengths, since calendar months and years vary slightly in actual day count.

Why do month and year use an average value?

A calendar month can range from 28 to 31 days, and a calendar year can be 365 or 366 days depending on leap years. To keep conversions consistent, these converters use the average Gregorian calendar length, the same approach used by most standard converters.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No, every calculation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript, nothing is uploaded or stored.

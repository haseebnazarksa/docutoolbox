Formula

What is a week?

A week is a unit of time equal to 7 days, widely used for scheduling recurring events, work cycles, and short-term planning.

What is a century?

A century is a unit of time equal to 100 years, commonly used to describe historical eras, long-term demographic data, and major milestones.

Common uses

  • Calculating age, tenure, or anniversaries across years and decades
  • Working out long-term financial projections such as loan terms or retirement planning
  • Studying historical timelines, generational spans, and demographic data
  • Estimating the lifespan of equipment, infrastructure, or long-term contracts

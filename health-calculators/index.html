Free health and fitness calculators

Docutoolbox's Health & Fitness Calculators cover the everyday numbers people look up most: body composition, calorie needs, hydration, pregnancy dates, and running pace. All 10 tools run entirely in your browser using JavaScript, nothing you enter is ever uploaded to a server.

How to choose the right tool

  • Checking your weight category from height and weight -> BMI Calculator
  • Finding how many calories you burn in a day -> BMR / TDEE Calculator
  • Setting a daily calorie target for weight loss -> Calorie Deficit Calculator
  • Figuring out how much water to drink -> Water Intake Calculator
  • Estimating body fat percentage with a tape measure -> Body Fat % Calculator
  • Splitting your calories into protein, carbs, and fat -> Macro Calculator
  • Comparing height-based weight formulas -> Ideal Body Weight Calculator
  • Estimating a pregnancy due date and current week -> Pregnancy Due Date Calculator
  • Timing bedtime or wake-up around sleep cycles -> Sleep Cycle Calculator
  • Working out running pace, time, or distance -> Pace & Running Calculator
These tools provide general estimates for informational purposes and are not medical advice. Individual health needs vary, consult a doctor, dietitian, or other qualified professional for guidance specific to you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use these calculators?

No, every tool is free with no sign-up, no email, and no limits on use.

Is my health data stored or sent anywhere?

No, all calculations happen locally in your browser using JavaScript, nothing you enter is transmitted to a server or stored.

Are these calculators accurate enough to replace a doctor?

No, they provide general estimates based on established formulas, but can't account for your full individual health picture. Use them as a starting reference, not a diagnosis or medical plan.

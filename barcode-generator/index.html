What is this tool?

This tool generates scannable 1D barcodes in several common formats used for retail products, shipping, and inventory. Type your data, choose a format, and the barcode renders instantly, ready to download as a PNG.

How to use this generator

  1. Enter your data (numbers or text, depending on format).
  2. Choose a format that matches your use case.
  3. The barcode updates live, download it once it looks correct.

Choosing a format

  • CODE128 - flexible, supports letters and numbers, widely used in shipping and logistics
  • EAN-13 - standard 13-digit retail product barcode used internationally
  • UPC-A - standard 12-digit retail barcode used mainly in the US and Canada
  • CODE39 - alphanumeric, common in inventory and ID badges
  • ITF-14 - used for shipping cartons and packaging

Frequently asked questions

Why does my barcode show an error?

Formats like EAN-13 and UPC-A require an exact number of digits (13 and 12 respectively), and only accept numeric input, check that your data matches the selected format's requirements.

Will this barcode work at retail checkout scanners?

Standard formats like EAN-13 and UPC-A are technically scannable, but real retail use requires officially registered barcode numbers (through GS1) to avoid duplicating another product's code.

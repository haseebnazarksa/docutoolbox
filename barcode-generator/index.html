What is a barcode generator?

A barcode generator is a digital tool that creates scannable barcode images from your data. It's used to manage inventory, track assets, streamline point-of-sale processes, and label products for shipping or retail.

How to use this generator

  1. Choose a barcode type from the dropdown, matching your use case.
  2. Enter your data, the text or numbers you want encoded.
  3. Review the live preview to make sure it looks correct.
  4. Download as PNG, JPG, or SVG for labels, packaging, or digital use.

Character limits by format

  • CODE128 - up to 80 alphanumeric characters
  • UPC-A - enter 11 digits, the 12th (check digit) is added automatically
  • EAN-13 - enter 12 digits, the 13th (check digit) is added automatically
  • CODE39 - up to 50 characters, uppercase letters, numbers, and select symbols
  • ITF-14 - enter 13 digits, the 14th (check digit) is added automatically

Choosing a format

  • CODE128 - flexible, supports letters and numbers, widely used in shipping and logistics
  • EAN-13 - standard 13-digit retail product barcode used internationally
  • UPC-A - standard 12-digit retail barcode used mainly in the US and Canada
  • CODE39 - alphanumeric, common in inventory and ID badges

Frequently asked questions

Can I use barcodes generated here on Amazon or other marketplaces?

Amazon and most major marketplaces require GS1-registered GTINs. Freely generated barcodes without a GS1 prefix may be rejected for retail distribution, for Amazon, Walmart, or similar platforms, purchase a registered GS1 barcode through GS1's official website.

Why does my barcode show an error?

Formats like EAN-13 and UPC-A only accept numeric input and a specific number of data digits, check that your entry matches the selected format's requirements listed above. The check digit is calculated for you automatically, so you don't need to work it out by hand.

Do I need to calculate the check digit myself?

No, for EAN-13, UPC-A, and ITF-14, this tool automatically calculates the correct check digit from your data digits using the standard mod-10 algorithm and appends it for you, shown live below the input field.

What's the difference between a 1D barcode and a QR code?

1D barcodes (like CODE128, EAN-13, UPC-A) encode data in a single row of parallel lines and are read by laser scanners. QR codes are 2D barcodes that store data in a grid pattern and hold far more information, use our separate QR Code Generator for those.

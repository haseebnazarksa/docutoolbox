What is the Passport Photo Resizer?

This tool crops and resizes a photo to the exact pixel dimensions required for a passport, visa, or standard ID photo. Choose your country or document type, position the crop box over your face, and download a photo sized correctly for printing or online submission.

Everything happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photo is never uploaded to a server, which matters for a photo of your own face.

How to use it

  1. Drop your photo onto the upload area, or click it to browse your device.
  2. Choose the photo type you need from the dropdown, each option locks the crop box to the correct aspect ratio.
  3. Drag the crop box to center your face, and drag the corner handles to resize it.
  4. Click Crop & download photo to get your correctly sized image.

Tips for a compliant passport photo

  • Use a plain, light-colored background if possible, many official requirements specify a white or off-white background
  • Face the camera directly with a neutral expression, most official guidelines require this
  • Make sure your face is well-lit and clearly visible, without shadows or glare
  • Center your face within the crop box, leaving appropriate space above your head and below your chin
  • Check your specific country or document's official requirements, since exact rules (background color, head size within frame, glasses policy) vary by issuing authority and can change over time

A note on official requirements

This tool crops your photo to the correct pixel dimensions for common document types, but it doesn't verify that your photo meets every official requirement, like background color, head positioning within the frame, or lighting. Always check your country or organization's current official photo guidelines before submitting, since requirements can be strict and vary by issuing authority.

Frequently asked questions

Will this photo be accepted for my passport application?

This tool ensures your photo has the correct pixel dimensions, but acceptance also depends on meeting other requirements like background, lighting, and expression set by your specific passport office. Always check your country's current official guidelines.

What DPI are these pixel sizes based on?

300 DPI, the standard resolution for print-quality photos. If you print the resulting image at that resolution, it will come out at the correct physical size in inches or millimeters.

Can I use this for other ID types not listed?

The Standard ID Card preset covers a common size, but if you need a very specific unusual size, the free-form Image Cropper tool lets you crop to any custom dimensions.

Does this tool check my background color or lighting?

No, it only handles cropping and resizing to the correct dimensions. You're responsible for taking a photo that meets your document's other requirements before uploading it here.

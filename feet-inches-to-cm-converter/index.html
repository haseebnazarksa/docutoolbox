How to convert feet and inches to cm

To convert feet and inches to centimeters, first convert the feet to inches (multiply by 12), add the extra inches, then multiply the total by 2.54. This calculator handles the full calculation instantly.

Worked example

Convert 5 ft 9 in to centimeters:
5 × 12 = 60 inches, + 9 inches = 69 total inches
69 × 2.54 = 175.26 cm

Formula

Total inches = (feet × 12) + inches, then cm = total inches × 2.54

What are feet and inches?

Feet and inches are imperial units of length, with 12 inches making up 1 foot. Together they form the standard way height is expressed in the US and UK, for example "5 feet 9 inches."

What is a centimeter?

A centimeter is a metric unit of length equal to one-hundredth of a meter. It's a common everyday unit for measuring height, small objects, and fabric.

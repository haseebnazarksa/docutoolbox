How to convert liters to pints

To convert a value from liters to pints, multiply it by 2.113379. This is a fixed, standardized US customary conversion factor, so the result is always mathematically exact, never an estimate.

What is a liter?

A liter is the base metric unit of volume, equal to 1,000 milliliters. It's used worldwide for bottled beverages, fuel, cooking recipes, and lab measurements.

What is a pint?

A pint is a US customary unit of volume equal to 2 cups, or one-eighth of a gallon. It's commonly used for measuring beer, milk, and other beverages.

Common uses

  • Reading product labels and container volumes in a different unit

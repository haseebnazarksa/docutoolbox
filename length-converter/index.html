Free length unit converters

Docutoolbox's Length Unit Converters cover the most common conversions between metric units (millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers) and imperial units (inches, feet, miles). Each converter has its own dedicated page for the fastest, most focused conversion experience, with live results, exact calculations, and a quick reference table.

How the converters work

Every conversion is calculated using internationally defined, exact conversion factors, not rounded approximations. Switching the "From" or "To" unit on any converter page instantly takes you to the matching dedicated converter, so you always land on the most relevant tool for your exact conversion.

Frequently asked questions

Are these conversions exact?

Yes, all length conversions here use fixed, internationally standardized conversion factors, results are mathematically exact.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No, every calculation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript, nothing is uploaded or stored.

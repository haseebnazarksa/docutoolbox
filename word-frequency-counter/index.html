What is the Word Frequency Counter?

This tool counts how many times each word appears in a block of text and ranks them from most to least frequent, along with what percentage of the total word count each represents. It's commonly used for keyword density analysis, writing style review, and text analysis.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. Choose whether to ignore case, exclude common filler words, and set a minimum word length to include.
  3. Click Analyze text to see the ranked frequency table.

Understanding the options

  • Ignore case - treats "The" and "the" as the same word
  • Exclude common words - filters out filler words like "the," "a," "is," and "and" that appear frequently in any text but carry little meaning
  • Minimum length - excludes very short words below the chosen character count

Common uses

  • Checking keyword density for SEO, making sure a target keyword appears often enough (but not excessively)
  • Reviewing your own writing for overused words or repetitive phrasing
  • Analyzing the most common terms in a document, speech, or set of survey responses
  • Building an intuition for a text's main themes at a glance

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a "common word"?

A built-in list of frequent English filler words (articles, prepositions, common pronouns and verbs like "the," "of," "is," "and") that typically appear often in any text without being meaningful keywords.

Does this handle punctuation correctly?

Yes, punctuation is stripped before counting, so "fox." and "fox," and "fox" are all counted as the same word.

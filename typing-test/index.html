What is the Typing Speed Test?

This tool measures how fast and how accurately you type. Choose a test duration, start typing the words shown, and get your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy percentage as soon as the timer runs out.

How to use it

  1. Pick a test duration: 15, 30, 60, or 120 seconds.
  2. Click the text area and start typing, the timer starts on your first keystroke, not before.
  3. Type each word exactly as shown, incorrect letters are marked as you go.
  4. When time runs out, your results appear automatically.
  5. Click Try again for a fresh set of words at the same duration.

How WPM and accuracy are calculated

WPM is calculated the standard way: total correctly typed characters divided by 5 (the average word length), divided by the time elapsed in minutes. Accuracy is the percentage of characters typed correctly out of all characters typed, including any mistakes.

Tips to improve your typing speed

  • Focus on accuracy first, speed naturally increases as accuracy improves
  • Keep your eyes on the screen, not your keyboard, to build muscle memory
  • Practice in short, regular sessions rather than occasional long ones
  • Use all ten fingers, with each finger responsible for a specific set of keys

Frequently asked questions

What is a good typing speed?

Average typing speed is around 40 WPM, professional typists often reach 65-75 WPM, and touch-typing experts can exceed 100 WPM.

Does the timer start immediately?

No, the timer only starts once you type your first character, so there's no rush to get ready.

Is my typing data stored anywhere?

No, everything happens locally in your browser, nothing you type is sent or saved anywhere.

What is this calculator?

This tool converts between running pace, time, and distance, useful for planning a race, checking training splits, or predicting your finish time for an upcoming event. Enter any two of the three values, distance, time, or pace, to solve for the third, and see predicted times for common race distances based on your current pace.

How to use this calculator

  1. Choose what you want to find: pace, finish time, or distance.
  2. Fill in the two known values.
  3. Click Calculate to see your result along with predicted finish times for 5K, 10K, half marathon, and marathon distances at that same pace.

Common uses

  • Working out what pace you need to hit a target race finish time
  • Predicting your finish time for an upcoming race based on recent training paces
  • Converting between minutes-per-km and minutes-per-mile pace
  • Planning even, negative, or interval splits for a race strategy

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the race distance predictions?

They assume you maintain the exact same pace across the full distance, which becomes less realistic for longer races, most runners naturally slow slightly over a marathon compared to a 5K pace, so treat longer-distance predictions as an optimistic estimate rather than a guarantee.

What's a good pace for a beginner?

This varies enormously by individual fitness, age, and experience, rather than a universal number, focus on a pace where you can hold a conversation comfortably (often called "easy pace") for most training runs, and gradually build speed from there.

What is this calculator?

This tool estimates how much water you should drink per day based on your body weight, how much you exercise, and the climate you're in. It's built on a commonly used baseline of roughly 30-35ml of water per kilogram of body weight, adjusted upward for exercise and heat, both of which increase fluid loss through sweat.

How to use this calculator

  1. Choose Metric or Imperial units and enter your weight.
  2. Select your typical activity level and the climate you live or exercise in.
  3. Click Calculate to see your recommended daily water intake, shown in both a total amount and glasses.

Why water needs vary by person

  • Body weight - larger bodies generally need more fluid to maintain normal function
  • Exercise - sweating during physical activity increases fluid loss that needs replacing
  • Climate - heat and humidity increase sweat loss even without exercise
  • Diet - this estimate doesn't account for water from food, which typically provides roughly 20% of total fluid intake
This tool provides a general estimate for informational purposes and is not medical advice. Water needs vary by individual health conditions, pregnancy, and medications, consult a doctor for personalized guidance, especially if you have kidney, heart, or other conditions affecting fluid balance.

Frequently asked questions

Does the "8 glasses a day" rule apply to everyone?

Not exactly, the 8-glasses guideline is a simplified rule of thumb, actual needs vary significantly based on body size, activity, and climate, which is why this calculator personalizes the estimate rather than giving one fixed number.

Do other drinks and food count toward water intake?

Yes, most beverages and water-rich foods (fruits, vegetables, soups) contribute to overall hydration, this calculator's number represents total fluid intake as a target, not strictly plain water.

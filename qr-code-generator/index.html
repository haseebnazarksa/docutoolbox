What is a QR code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores data, like a URL, text, or Wi-Fi credentials, in a pattern that any smartphone camera can scan instantly. This tool generates a QR code image for several common use cases, ready to download and use.

How to use this generator

  1. Choose a type: URL, Text, Wi-Fi, or Contact.
  2. Fill in the relevant fields, the QR code updates live as you type.
  3. Adjust size and colors if needed.
  4. Click Download PNG to save the image.

Common uses

  • Linking a printed flyer, menu, or business card to a website
  • Sharing Wi-Fi access without typing out a password
  • Adding a scannable contact card to an email signature or namecard
  • Directing people to a form, document, or promotional page

Frequently asked questions

Will this QR code expire or stop working?

No, these are static QR codes that encode your data directly, they don't expire and don't require any account or service to keep working.

What's the maximum amount of text a QR code can hold?

QR codes can hold up to a few thousand characters, but very long text produces a denser, harder-to-scan code, shorter content (like a URL) scans more reliably.

What are macros?

Macronutrients, or "macros", are the three nutrients that provide calories: protein, carbohydrates, and fat. Protein and carbs provide about 4 calories per gram, while fat provides about 9 calories per gram. This calculator splits your total daily calorie target into grams of each, based on a percentage split you choose.

How to use this calculator

  1. If you don't know your daily calorie target, check it first with the BMR/TDEE Calculator.
  2. Enter your daily calorie target.
  3. Choose a macro split, or select Custom to set your own percentages.
  4. Click Calculate to see grams and percentages for protein, carbs, and fat.

Common macro splits

  • Balanced (30/40/30) - a general-purpose split suitable for most goals
  • High protein (40/30/30) - often used to support muscle retention during a calorie deficit, or muscle building
  • Low carb (35/25/40) - reduces carbohydrate intake in favor of more fat and moderate protein

There's no single "correct" macro split, the ideal ratio depends on your goals, activity level, and personal preference, and what you can realistically sustain long-term matters more than hitting an exact ratio.

This tool provides general estimates for informational purposes and is not medical or nutritional advice. Individual macro needs vary based on health conditions, training, and goals, consult a registered dietitian for a personalized nutrition plan.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to hit my macros exactly every day?

No, being reasonably close on most days matters far more than hitting exact numbers daily, treat these targets as a guide rather than a strict requirement.

Which macro split is best for weight loss?

Higher protein splits are often recommended during a calorie deficit, since adequate protein helps preserve muscle mass while losing fat, but total calorie intake matters more for weight loss than the specific macro ratio.

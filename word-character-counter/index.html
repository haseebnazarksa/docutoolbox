What is the Word & Character Counter?

This tool counts words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in any text, updating live as you type or paste. It also estimates reading time, useful for keeping blog posts, essays, captions, and ad copy within a target length.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Every stat updates instantly below as you edit.
  3. Use Copy text to copy your content, or Clear to start over.

Understanding the stats

  • Words - counted the same way as Microsoft Word and Google Docs, splitting on whitespace
  • Characters - every character including spaces and punctuation
  • Characters (no spaces) - useful for platforms with strict character limits
  • Sentences - counted by sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ?)
  • Paragraphs - counted by line breaks
  • Reading time - estimated at 200 words per minute, the average adult silent reading speed

Common uses

  • Checking an essay or article against a required word count
  • Staying within social media character limits
  • Estimating how long a blog post or script will take to read
  • Checking meta description length for SEO

Frequently asked questions

Does this count hyphenated words as one word or two?

Hyphenated words like "well-known" are counted as a single word, matching the convention used by Microsoft Word and most publishers.

Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

No, everything happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type is transmitted to a server.

What is the Height Converter?

This tool converts height between the metric system (centimeters) and the imperial system (feet and inches), the two formats most commonly used worldwide. It includes quick-select buttons for common heights, and a reference table showing typical height values in both formats.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose a direction: CM to Feet & Inches, or Feet & Inches to CM.
  2. Enter the height, or click a quick-select preset.
  3. The converted result updates instantly.

Common uses

  • Filling out international forms, applications, or medical records that require a specific height format
  • Comparing height in a format you're more familiar with
  • Sports, fitness, and clothing sizing that uses different regional height conventions
  • Travel and international communication

Frequently asked questions

Which countries use feet and inches vs centimeters?

The US and, to a lesser extent, the UK commonly use feet and inches for height, while most of the rest of the world uses centimeters as the standard.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes, based on the exact definition that 1 inch equals 2.54 cm, results are mathematically precise, not approximations.

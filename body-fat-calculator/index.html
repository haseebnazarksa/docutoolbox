What is the US Navy method?

The US Navy body fat method estimates body fat percentage using circumference measurements (neck, waist, and for women, hips) combined with height, rather than requiring calipers, scans, or specialized equipment. It was developed for military fitness assessments and remains one of the most accessible and widely used estimation methods available.

How to use this calculator

  1. Select your sex.
  2. Measure your neck (just below the larynx), waist (at the navel), and if you're a woman, your hips (at the widest point), using a flexible tape measure.
  3. Enter your height and measurements.
  4. Click Calculate to see your estimated body fat percentage and category.

Body fat percentage categories

General ranges (these vary by source and individual factors like age):

  • Men: Essential fat 2-5%, Athletes 6-13%, Fitness 14-17%, Average 18-24%, Above average 25%+
  • Women: Essential fat 10-13%, Athletes 14-20%, Fitness 21-24%, Average 25-31%, Above average 32%+
This is an estimation method with a typical margin of error of a few percentage points compared to lab methods like DEXA scans or hydrostatic weighing. It provides a general estimate for informational purposes and is not medical advice. Measurement technique significantly affects accuracy, measure consistently and consult a professional for precise body composition analysis.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this compared to a DEXA scan?

Less precise, tape-measurement methods like this one typically have a margin of error of around 3-4 percentage points compared to gold-standard methods like DEXA scans, but they're far more accessible and useful for tracking relative changes over time.

Where exactly should I measure my waist?

At the navel, for the most consistent results, measure at the same time of day (ideally in the morning, before eating) and in the same conditions each time you check.

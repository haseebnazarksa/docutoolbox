Free area unit converters

Docutoolbox's Area Unit Converters cover every conversion between square millimeters, square centimeters, square meters, square kilometers, hectares, acres, square inches, square feet, square yards, and square miles. Each converter has its own dedicated page for the fastest, most focused conversion experience, with live results, the exact formula, and a quick reference table.

How the converters work

Every area converter on this page uses a ratio-based conversion engine. Each unit is converted through a fixed factor relative to the square meter, the SI base unit of area, so results are always mathematically exact rather than approximated. Switching the "From" or "To" unit on any converter page instantly takes you to the matching dedicated converter, so you always land on the most relevant tool for your exact conversion.

Frequently asked questions

Are these conversions exact?

Yes, all area conversions here use fixed, standardized conversion factors, results are mathematically exact.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No, every calculation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript, nothing is uploaded or stored.

What is a square kilometer?

A square kilometer is a large metric unit of area, defined as the area of a square with sides one kilometer in length. It's used for measuring cities, regions, and large geographic areas.

What is a square meter?

A square meter is the SI derived unit of area, defined as the area of a square with sides one meter in length. It's the standard unit of area used worldwide for real estate, land, and construction measurements.

Common uses

  • Measuring farmland, plots, and large estates
  • Comparing land area across countries and regions
  • Urban planning and zoning calculations
  • Agricultural and forestry land management

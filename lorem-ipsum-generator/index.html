What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text derived from a passage of classical Latin, used by designers and developers since the 1500s to fill layouts with realistic-looking text before real content is ready. Because it reads as gibberish rather than a real language, it doesn't distract from evaluating a design's visual layout, typography, and spacing.

How to use it

  1. Choose whether to generate by paragraphs, sentences, or words.
  2. Enter the amount you need.
  3. Optionally keep the classic opening line, "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet..."
  4. Click Generate text and copy the result.

Why designers still use it

Lorem Ipsum lets you evaluate a layout's visual balance, line length, and typography without the distraction of reading actual content, which can bias how a design is judged. It also produces a realistic mix of short and long words, similar to real language, unlike simpler placeholders like repeated "text text text."

Common uses

  • Filling in mockups and wireframes before real copy is written
  • Testing how a website or app layout handles varying content lengths
  • Populating a CMS or template during development
  • Demonstrating a font or typographic style

Frequently asked questions

What does Lorem Ipsum actually mean?

It's derived from a scrambled passage of Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum," a 1st century BC Latin text on ethics. The words are altered and rearranged, so it doesn't form coherent Latin sentences with real meaning.

Can I generate a very large amount of text?

Yes, up to 50 paragraphs, sentences, or words at once, sufficient for most mockup and testing purposes.

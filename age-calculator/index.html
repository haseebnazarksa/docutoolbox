Frequently asked
How does this age calculator work?
It compares your date of birth to today's date and works out the exact difference in years, months, and days — correctly accounting for leap years and varying month lengths.
Is my date of birth stored anywhere?
No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server or saved. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Can I check someone else's age too?
Yes — the calculator works for any valid date of birth, not just your own.

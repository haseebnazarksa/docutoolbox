What is the Age Calculator?

The Age Calculator finds your exact age from a date of birth — not just in years, but broken down into years, months, and days, along with your total age in weeks, days, hours, minutes, and even a live-updating seconds counter. It also shows exactly how far away your next birthday is, down to the day.

Everything runs live in your browser. Your date of birth is never sent anywhere or stored — the moment you close the page, it's gone.

How to use it

  1. Select your birth day, month, and year from the dropdowns.
  2. Click "Calculate my age".
  3. Your exact age appears broken down into years, months, and days, along with a running live counter of the seconds you've been alive.
  4. Scroll down for the same age expressed in weeks, total days, hours, and minutes, plus a countdown to your next birthday.

How the age calculation works

The calculator compares your date of birth to today's date and works out the exact difference in years, months, and days — correctly handling varying month lengths and leap years along the way. Unlike a simple "current year minus birth year" subtraction, it checks whether your birthday has already happened this year to make sure the year count is accurate right up to the day.

The "total days," "weeks," "hours," and "minutes" figures are calculated from the exact number of milliseconds between your birth date and the current moment, converted into each unit.

How the next-birthday countdown works

The calculator finds the next occurrence of your birth month and day — this year if it hasn't happened yet, or next year if it has already passed — and counts the days remaining until it arrives. The progress ring visualizes how far you are through your current "birthday year," filling up as your next birthday approaches.

Worked example

If you were born on March 15, 2000, and today is August 9, 2026, you'd be 26 years, 4 months, and 25 days old. Your next birthday would fall on March 15, 2027 — about 7 months away — and the calculator would also show your age in total days (roughly 9,650), weeks (roughly 1,378), and hours (roughly 231,600).

Common uses

  • Quickly checking your exact age for a form or application
  • Finding out how many days remain until your next birthday
  • Checking someone else's age from their date of birth
  • Satisfying curiosity about milestones — how many days, hours, or seconds you've been alive
  • Verifying age eligibility requirements for events, memberships, or applications

Frequently asked questions

How does this age calculator work?

It compares your date of birth to today's date and works out the exact difference in years, months, and days — correctly accounting for leap years and varying month lengths.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server or saved. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I check someone else's age too?

Yes — the calculator works for any valid date of birth, not just your own.

Why does the "seconds lived" counter keep changing?

It updates once per second in real time, since the exact number of seconds you've been alive is constantly increasing.
